🐿️ Squirreling Around Campus

An interactive behavioral ecology investigation • simulated animals and environments

Research question

How do habitat, distance to safety, and human activity affect squirrel vigilance and foraging?

This is a synthetic teaching simulation, not actual field footage or data from Squirrel-Net. Behavioral probabilities are designed to illustrate ecological trade-offs; they are not empirical estimates for Sciurus niger.

Protocol: Five-minute focal follows, instantaneous scan samples at 0:00, 0:20, …, 4:40 (15 observations per trial). Choose one behavior at each beep. Each trial represents one simulated squirrel. Complete 8 trials (2 habitats × 2 cover distances × 2 replicates), with optional additional trials.
